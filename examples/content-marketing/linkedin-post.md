# Your best LinkedIn post may have a second life as audio

Daniel Rosehill’s talking to his camera about a marketing idea: don’t make people follow a channel just to reach your content.

For a marketing agency, that shifts the question from “Which channel should we choose?” to “How else can clients consume what we already make?”

Rosehill suggests bridging text, audio and video. Start with a blog post, for example, then add an audio version and embed a video. Or publish a video and link to its text readout or podcast version.

The point isn’t to be everywhere. It’s to make useful content available in formats that suit your audience, then connect those versions.

Pick one strong piece of client content. What’s one other format you could offer?
